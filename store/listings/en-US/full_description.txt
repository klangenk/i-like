I Like is your personal memory for everything you love – and everything you never want again. The rating app for products, groceries, wine, beer, books, board games, movies and restaurants.

Your ratings belong to you: everything stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Whether it's a café, a pizza, a book, a board game or something from the supermarket: with I Like you capture in seconds what you thought of it. Instead of stars you rate in words – Nope, Meh, Okay, Good or Love. So next time you shop or eat out, you know right away what's worth it: scan something you've already rated and the app shows your verdict straight away.

QUICK TO ADD
• Scan a barcode – product and book details load automatically
• Share a link – straight from your browser or shops like Amazon
• Find books (Open Library) and board games, with covers right in the results
• Take your own photo – the app suggests a title and category; text recognition runs on your device
• Or simply enter it yourself
• Haven't tried it yet? Put it on your "to try" list and rate it later

EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
• Photo grid of all your ratings
• Filter by level: just your favourites – or everything that was "Nope"
• Author, maker or brand and year for every rating
• Sort by date, rating or name, group and filter by category – including your own categories
• Share ratings with friends as an image card
• Search across titles, makers, categories and notes
• Your balance: how your ratings are spread

PERFECT FOR
• Shopping: which yogurt, coffee or sunscreen was good?
• Wine and beer ratings without a notebook
• A reading log and your board game collection
• Remembering restaurants, cafés and dishes
• A wishlist of things you still want to try

YOUR DATA
• All ratings and photos stay on your device
• Optional: encrypted backup to your own Google account, for a new phone
• Export as JSON or CSV, import from JSON
• Light and dark mode, in German and English
